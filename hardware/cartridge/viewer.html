How it attaches

The underside is a saddle: a channel as thick as the PSP (23 mm plus 0.3 mm of play per side) with a 6 mm lip over the screen side and a 12 mm lip over the back. A 0.5 mm bump along each lip clicks over the faces and holds it. The front lip stays inside the bezel above the screen.

The Mini-B plug sits in the saddle's roof, in a pocket with 0.4 mm of float, so it lines up with the port by itself. The saddle carries the cartridge's weight and any knock, not the PSP's USB socket.

Cut through the plug to see the saddle's profile around the PSP.

Inside

  • Front layer: the Pi Zero 2 W on four 3 mm standoffs, chips facing inward. The microSD card is reachable through a slot in the left end.
  • Middle layer: the plug cradle at the bottom, the charger module at the top right with its USB-C port in the top face, and vents over the Pi's processor.
  • Back layer: a 1000 mAh LiPo (50 × 34 × 5 mm), about 3 hours of play (an estimate, to be measured).
  • Four wires go from the plug to the Pi's USB test pads. The Pi is the USB host and supplies the PSP's VBUS from its 5 V rail.

To measure on a real PSP-1000

These are estimates in the model. Each one is a single number in cartridge.py:

  • USB_X: where the Mini-B port sits along the top edge (the cartridge centres on it).
  • USB_Z: where the port sits across the thickness.
  • SHOULDER_IN: where L ends and R starts. The cartridge is 84 mm wide.
  • BEZEL_TOP: the front face above the screen glass (the front lip must stay above it).
  • The thickness at the top edge where the lips sit, and how round the top edges are.

Parts to buy

  • Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W
  • LiPo 503450, 3.7 V 1000 mAh, with a protection board
  • USB-C charger and 5 V boost module, up to 23 × 36 × 5 mm
  • Mini-B male plug, solder type
  • Slide switch, 8.6 × 3.8 mm body (SS12D00 class)
  • 4 × M2 × 8 screws, 4 × M2 heat-set inserts (3.2 mm), 4 × M2.5 × 4 self-tapping screws

Print both halves in PETG, outer face down, 0.2 mm layers. The shell is about 31 cm³; the finished cartridge weighs roughly 75 g, against the PSP's 280 g.
